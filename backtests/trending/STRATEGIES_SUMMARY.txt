================================================================================
TRENDING STRATEGIES COLLECTION - MOON DEV'S TRADING BOTS
================================================================================

This collection contains 12 trending strategies organized into 4 categories:
- Slow Trend Following (Long Only)
- Slow Trend Following (Long and Short)
- Slow Trend Following with Trend Strength
- Fast Trend Following with Trend Strength

All strategies use the backtesting.py framework with talib indicators and
support multi-data testing across 30+ data sources.

================================================================================
CATEGORY 1: SLOW TREND FOLLOWING - LONG ONLY (3 Strategies)
================================================================================

1. MA200 Long Trend (trending_ma200_long.py)
   - Entry: Price crosses above 200-day MA
   - Exit: Price falls below 200-day MA, take profit (15%), or stop loss (8%)
   - Best for: Long-term trend following in trending markets
   - Time horizon: Daily/Weekly timeframes

2. Golden Cross Trend (trending_golden_cross.py)
   - Entry: 50-day MA crosses above 200-day MA (golden cross)
   - Exit: Death cross, take profit (20%), or stop loss (10%)
   - Best for: Major trend reversals and strong bull markets
   - Time horizon: Daily timeframes

3. Donchian Long Trend (trending_donchian_long.py)
   - Entry: Price breaks above 20-period Donchian Channel upper band
   - Exit: Price falls below middle band, take profit (12%), or stop loss (6%)
   - Best for: Breakout trading in trending markets
   - Time horizon: Any timeframe

================================================================================
CATEGORY 2: SLOW TREND FOLLOWING - LONG AND SHORT (3 Strategies)
================================================================================

4. Dual MA Trend (trending_dual_ma.py)
   - Long: MA50 > MA200
   - Short: MA50 < MA200
   - Exit: Trend reversal, take profit (15%), or stop loss (8%)
   - Best for: Capturing both bull and bear trends
   - Time horizon: Daily timeframes

5. RSI-MA Trend (trending_rsi_ma.py)
   - Long: MA50 > MA200 AND RSI > 50
   - Short: MA50 < MA200 AND RSI < 50
   - Exit: Conditions no longer met, take profit (12%), or stop loss (6%)
   - Best for: Trend following with momentum confirmation
   - Time horizon: Daily/4H timeframes

6. Bollinger Trend (trending_bollinger_trend.py)
   - Long: Break above upper band in uptrend (price > MA50)
   - Short: Break below lower band in downtrend (price < MA50)
   - Exit: Price returns to middle band, take profit (10%), or stop loss (5%)
   - Best for: Volatility breakout trading
   - Time horizon: Any timeframe

================================================================================
CATEGORY 3: SLOW TREND WITH TREND STRENGTH (3 Strategies)
================================================================================

7. ADX-MA Trend (trending_adx_ma.py)
   - Entry: MA crossovers filtered by ADX > 25
   - Long: Golden cross with strong trend (+DI > -DI)
   - Short: Death cross with strong trend (-DI > +DI)
   - Exit: ADX < 20, trend reversal, take profit (15%), or stop loss (8%)
   - Best for: Trading only in strong trending markets
   - Time horizon: Daily timeframes

8. MACD-ADX Trend (trending_macd_adx.py)
   - Entry: MACD crossovers filtered by ADX > 25
   - Long: MACD crosses above signal with positive histogram
   - Short: MACD crosses below signal with negative histogram
   - Exit: MACD reversal, ADX < 20, take profit (12%), or stop loss (6%)
   - Best for: Momentum trading in strong trends
   - Time horizon: Daily/4H timeframes

9. Volume-MA Trend (trending_volume_ma.py)
   - Entry: MA crossovers with volume confirmation (1.5x average)
   - Long: Golden cross with volume spike
   - Short: Death cross with volume spike
   - Exit: Trend reversal, take profit (14%), or stop loss (7%)
   - Best for: High conviction trend changes
   - Time horizon: Daily timeframes

================================================================================
CATEGORY 4: FAST TREND WITH TREND STRENGTH (3 Strategies)
================================================================================

10. Fast MA-ADX Trend (trending_fast_ma_adx.py)
    - Entry: 10/30 MA crossovers filtered by ADX > 25
    - Long: Fast MA crosses above slow MA with +DI > -DI
    - Short: Fast MA crosses below slow MA with -DI > +DI
    - Exit: MA reversal, ADX < 20, take profit (8%), or stop loss (4%)
    - Best for: Short-term trend trading
    - Time horizon: 15min/1H timeframes

11. Fast BB-ADX Trend (trending_fast_bb_adx.py)
    - Entry: Bollinger Band breaks with ADX > 25
    - Long: Break above upper band with +DI > -DI
    - Short: Break below lower band with -DI > +DI
    - Exit: Return to middle band, ADX < 20, take profit (6%), or stop loss (3%)
    - Best for: Intraday volatility breakouts
    - Time horizon: 5min/15min timeframes

12. Fast MACD-ADX Trend (trending_fast_macd_adx.py)
    - Entry: Fast MACD (6,13,5) crossovers with ADX > 25
    - Long: MACD crosses above signal with positive histogram
    - Short: MACD crosses below signal with negative histogram
    - Exit: Histogram reversal, ADX < 20, take profit (7%), or stop loss (3.5%)
    - Best for: Quick momentum trades
    - Time horizon: 5min/15min/1H timeframes

================================================================================
USAGE INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================

1. To run a single strategy:
   python trending_ma200_long.py

2. To test all strategies:
   python test_all_strategies.py

3. Results are saved in ./results/ directory as CSV files

4. Each strategy supports multi-data testing across:
   - Bitcoin (multiple timeframes)
   - Ethereum (multiple timeframes)
   - Solana (multiple timeframes)
   - Stock data from Interactive Brokers
   - Yahoo Finance hourly data

================================================================================
PARAMETER OPTIMIZATION NOTES
================================================================================

All strategies have adjustable parameters that can be optimized:
- Take profit and stop loss levels
- Moving average periods
- ADX thresholds
- Bollinger Band periods and standard deviations
- MACD periods
- Volume multipliers

Use backtesting.py's optimization features to find optimal parameters
for specific markets and timeframes.
