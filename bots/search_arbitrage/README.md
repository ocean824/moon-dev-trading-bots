## 📊 What is Search Arbitrage?

Search arbitrage is a digital marketing strategy that involves:

1. Buying traffic (clicks) from one platform at a lower cost
2. Directing that traffic to content pages with search ads
3. Selling those clicks at a higher price to advertisers
4. Profiting from the difference

**The Basic Formula:**
- Buy clicks for $0.50 (Facebook, TikTok, etc.)
- Sell clicks for $0.60+ (Google, etc.)
- Profit $0.10+ per click

With proper scaling, this can generate **$100k/MO** with the right systems in place.

## 🌐 Understanding the Market

### RSOC vs AFD

- **RSOC (Related Search Organic Content)**: The current standard in search arbitrage. Requires one less click than AFD and provides higher advertiser quality.
- **AFD (Arbitrage Funnel Design)**: An older product with lower margins.

### Key Players

- **Feed Providers**: Tonic, System1, Domain Active, Sedo, Ads.com
- **Tracking Platforms**: ClickFlare.io, Maximizer, RedTrack.io
- **Creative Services**: Trendzi.io

### Revenue Models

- Focus on RPM (Revenue Per Mille) rather than just CPC
- Net-30 payment terms are common (plan cash flow accordingly)
- Margins typically range from 10-20% but can be higher with optimization

## 🏗️ Setting Up Your Operation

### Step 1: Research & Planning

1. **Choose Your Vertical**:
   - Dental implants
   - Health conditions (e.g., Hepatitis C)
   - Job opportunities
   - Cleaning services
   - Financial services (loans, insurance)

2. **Keyword Research**:
   ```
   📝 Moon Dev's Keyword Research Process 📝
   1. Use Google Ads Keyword Planner
   2. Enter main keyword → Discover keywords
   3. Note top of page bid (good indicator of value)
   4. Use SEMrush to sort by volume
   5. AVOID brand keywords (not allowed in search arb)
   ```

3. **Competitor Analysis**:
   - Study Facebook/TikTok ad libraries
   - Look at oldest running ads (indicates what works)
   - Check privacy policies on RSOC pages to identify networks

### Step 2: Account Setup

1. **Ad Accounts**:
   - Create accounts on Facebook, TikTok, Snapchat
   - Start with small budgets ($5-10) and scale gradually
   - Consider account duplication for scale

2. **Feed Provider Relationships**:
   - Contact Tonic, System1, Domain Active, etc.
   - Ask for high-payout, low-competition niches
   - Negotiate terms based on volume

3. **Tracking Setup**:
   - Implement ClickFlare or Maximizer
   - Set up proper attribution models
   - Create stop-loss systems that auto-pause if CPA targets aren't met
   - buyild custom tools to get an edge

## 💻 Traffic Sources Strategy

### Facebook/Instagram

- Use sales campaigns
- UGC (User Generated Content) performs best
- Test multiple creatives with small budgets
- Scale winners gradually

### TikTok

```
🔥 Moon Dev's TikTok Strategy 🔥
- Focus on authentic, native-feeling content
- Use green screen effects (can reduce CPC from $0.50 to $0.15)
- Launch thousands of ads with tiny budgets
- Test different creators, angles, hooks, and CTAs
```
