# personal algo

in order to acheive anything in life, we must make sure that our personal algorithm is tuned

quant is one of the hardest games in the world

and in order to even compete, we must make sure that our actions align with our goals

so we must ensure that our thoughts align with our goals too

as we are simply our thoughts

so how do we ensure that every day, we take the correct step forward to acheive our goals

1. we must have fun
2. we must have energy
3. we must be healthy
4. we must have a plan (algo)
5. we must have discipline to execute that plan every day

but life has distractions, and things to get you off your algo

just like trading, there are things that will speed you up and get you rekt (leverage, flip flopping positons etc.)

the goal is not to make it over night

the goal is to develop an algo that ensures your make it over time

as long as your personal algo is ensuring your thoughts line up with your goals

if you follow that for long enough (and dont flip) you can acheive anything
