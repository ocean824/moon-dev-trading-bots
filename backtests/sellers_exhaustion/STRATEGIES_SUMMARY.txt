SELLER EXHAUSTION STRATEGIES COLLECTION
========================================
Created: 2025-08-23
Location: /Users/md/Dropbox/dev/github/moon-dev-trading-bots/backtests/sellers_exhaustion/

All strategies are long-only, looking for exhaustion bottoms with:
- 3% take profit target
- 2% stop loss
- Multi-data testing capability across 29+ data sources

STRATEGIES IMPLEMENTED:
-----------------------

1. sellers_exhaustion_volume_wick.py
   - Detects exhaustion when sellers dump large volume but price fails to make new lows
   - Looks for volume spikes (2x average) with long lower wicks (>50% of candle range)
   - Confirms with price near recent lows

2. sellers_exhaustion_climax.py
   - Identifies volume climax bars with 2.5x average volume on new lows that quickly rebound
   - Detects when price rebounds from the low (closes in upper 70% of range)
   - Additional confirmation from being at new lows

3. sellers_exhaustion_declining_volume.py
   - Detects when price makes lower lows but on decreasing volume (sellers tiring)
   - Volume must be <70% of previous low's volume
   - Uses RSI <35 for additional oversold confirmation

4. sellers_exhaustion_volatility.py
   - ATR spikes (1.5x average) on big red candles then contracts while price tries to push lower
   - Detects failed breakdown attempts with declining volume
   - Volatility expansion followed by contraction pattern

5. sellers_exhaustion_obv.py
   - OBV or A/D line diverging upward while price trends down
   - Uses normalized indicators to detect bullish divergence
   - Requires 2% price decline with positive indicator slope
   - RSI <40 for confirmation

6. sellers_exhaustion_sequential.py
   - Counts consecutive down closes (4-9 range) before volume spike and reversal
   - Looks for volume spike (2x average) after sequential declines
   - Detects reversal candle patterns (hammer, doji, bullish engulfing)
   - RSI <35 for oversold confirmation

7. sellers_exhaustion_mfi_bb.py
   - Uses Money Flow Index (MFI) <25 for oversold conditions
   - Price at or below lower Bollinger Band
   - ADX <25 indicates weak trend (exhaustion more likely)
   - Volume confirmation for reversal

RUNNING THE STRATEGIES:
-----------------------
Each strategy can be run individually:
    python sellers_exhaustion_volume_wick.py
    python sellers_exhaustion_climax.py
    (etc.)

Or test all strategies:
    python test_all_strategies.py

Results are saved to ./results/ directory as CSV files.

TECHNICAL INDICATORS USED:
--------------------------
- Volume Moving Average (SMA)
- RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- OBV (On-Balance Volume)
- A/D (Accumulation/Distribution)
- ATR (Average True Range)
- MFI (Money Flow Index)
- Bollinger Bands
- ADX (Average Directional Index)
- ROC (Rate of Change)

All strategies use talib for indicator calculations and follow the backtesting.py framework.
